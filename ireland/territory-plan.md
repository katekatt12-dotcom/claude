## 1. Executive summary

Ireland's pharma capital cycle in 2026–27 is dominated by **biologics drug substance**, **GLP-1 / obesity oral & sterile capacity**, and **bioprocessing consumables** — all of which sit directly on top of Orise's offering (SCADA, MES, data historian/AVEVA PI, serialization, IT/OT convergence, automation, and CSV/qualification under GAMP 5 / 21 CFR Part 11 / cGMP).

**The shape of the opportunity:**

- **Two mega-projects anchor the territory:** Pfizer Grange Castle (€1.2bn biologics DS, commissioning 2027) and Eli Lilly Raheen/Limerick (~$2bn biologics API, production start 2026). Both hit their commissioning/qualification peak *inside* the window — the exact moment CSV, MES, historian and automation spend is committed.
- **GLP-1/obesity is a distinct sub-wave:** Lilly Kinsale ($800m, tirzepatide) and **Novo Nordisk Athlone (€432m oral GLP-1, announced 2 March 2026)** — the freshest large project in the dataset, with construction live now.
- **A cluster of €350–500m programmes** ramps through the window: MSD Dundalk (ex-WuXi, >€500m), Merck KGaA Cork membranes/filtration (€440m), Amgen Dún Laoghaire ($407m aseptic).
- **A mid-tier of €36–200m biologics builds** offers faster, lower-friction entry: Regeneron Raheen, BioMarin Shanbally, Alexion (AstraZeneca) Dublin/Athlone, Takeda cell therapy, AbbVie Cork sterile.
- **Four majors are DIVESTING or CLOSED in Ireland** — Novartis (→Sterling), GSK (→Thermo Fisher), Roche (Clarecastle closure/sale), and a Merck KGaA API plant (mothball by 2028). These flip the opportunity to the **incoming CDMOs** (Sterling Pharma Solutions, Thermo Fisher) — worth adding to Orise's target list.

## 2. Detailed project descriptions

### Tier 1 — Anchor projects (large, in-window commissioning)

**Pfizer — Grange Castle, Dublin — €1.2bn (US$1.26bn)**
New facility to *double* the site's biological drug-substance capacity (large-molecule/mAbs, vaccines). Construction from 2024; **final commissioning 2027**; up to 500 jobs (Ireland total ~5,500). A separate initial-stage clinical/development manufacturing facility is part of the same package at Ringaskiddy, Cork. This is Pfizer's largest-ever Irish investment. *Confidence: High.* Sources: BioProcess Intl, IDA, Pharmaceutical-Technology.

**Eli Lilly — Raheen Business Park, Limerick — ~US$2bn**
Greenfield biologics drug-substance (API) plant on Lilly's biotech campus; €400m original (2022) + $1bn expansion (Sept 2024) + reported additional $500m. Produces monoclonal antibodies including the Alzheimer's portfolio (donanemab/Kisunla). **Biologic active-ingredient production on track to start 2026**; ~450 jobs; billed as Lilly's most technically advanced mAb site (35% lower energy intensity). *Confidence: High.* Sources: Lilly investor release, Pharmaceutical-Technology, FiercePharma.

**Eli Lilly — Kinsale, Co. Cork — US$800m**
Part of the same $1.8bn Sept-2024 package. Capacity for diabetes/obesity therapies (tirzepatide — Mounjaro/Zepbound). Began making medicines in 2025; scale-up/ramp active 2026–27. A May-2026 Irish Times feature confirms the site at the centre of Lilly's global weight-loss supply. *Confidence: High.*

**Novo Nordisk — Monksland, Athlone — €432m (US$506m)**
**Announced 2 March 2026** — the freshest large project in the dataset. A tabletting facility for **oral GLP-1s (the Wegovy/semaglutide pill)**: upgrade/retrofit of the existing plant plus new capacity. Construction already begun; completing gradually end-2027 through 2028; 260 operational staff + up to 500–600 construction jobs. *Confidence: High.* Sources: RTÉ, IDA, PharmExec.

**MSD (Merck & Co.) — Dundalk, Co. Louth — >€500m (US$520m)**
Acquisition of the former WuXi Vaccines plant (15,520 m², vaccine drug substance + drug product + QC labs). Deal announced Jan 2025, handover H1 2025; +150 staff by end-2025; part of MSD's plan to add ~1,000 jobs across 8 Irish sites. Integration, automation and ramp of the existing plant run through 2026–27. *Confidence: High (purchase price well-sourced).* The €1bn Carlow/Dunboyne builds already opened in 2023 (context only, outside window).

### Tier 2 — Mid-large programmes ramping through the window

**Merck KGaA (MilliporeSigma) — Carrigtwohill & Blarney, Cork — €440m**
Bioprocessing consumables: Blarney €150m climate-neutral **filtration** facility opened Sept 2025 (production Q4 2025); Carrigtwohill €290m **membrane** plant (immersion casting for gene/novel therapies, virus filtration) — first commercial output 2026; whole programme wraps 2027; 370+ jobs. *Confidence: High.* (Separate negative: a different Merck KGaA Irish API plant to be mothballed by end-2028 — clarify which.)

**Amgen — Dún Laoghaire, Dublin — US$407m (€353m) capex 2023–24**
Installation, construction and testing of manufacturing facilities at the 32-acre aseptic drug-product site (filling, lyophilization, formulation, packaging, analytical testing). Workforce growing toward ~2,888 by 2035. *Confidence: High.* (Watch: Amgen to divest an ex-Horizon aseptic plant by 2026 — verify Irish relevance.)

**Regeneron — Raheen, Limerick — €100m confirmed (Oct 2025) / ~€200m planned**
Ireland's largest-scale bulk-biologics site (IOPS, 48,000 m²). €100m + 300 jobs announced Oct 2025; a larger physical expansion (+240,000 sq ft plus a new R&D building) reported at ~€200m. >€1bn spent at the site over five years. *Confidence: High* (clarify whether €100m and €200m are the same or sequential programmes).

### Tier 3 — Focused biologics / specialty builds (fast entry)

**Alexion (AstraZeneca) — College Park & Blanchardstown, Dublin + Monksland, Athlone — €65m**
New drug-substance production equipment + a Manufacturing Sciences & Technology (MS&T) lab + cold/ambient warehousing; 105 jobs; ~25-month build; **operational 2026 after commissioning**. Part of AstraZeneca's ~$360m Irish programme. *Confidence: High.*

**BioMarin — Shanbally, Cork — €60m lab + >€30m packaging**
Only BioMarin plant outside the US; end-to-end biologics (bulk DS → drug product → fill-finish → packaging). €60m four-storey QC/process-development lab to lift capacity; packaging expansion; 40+ roles ongoing. *Confidence: High.*

**Takeda — Grange Castle, Dublin — €36m**
Ireland's first commercial-scale **cell therapy** production facility; ~100 jobs over three years; ramp/qualification active into 2026. Small capex but high technical value (complex, data-intensive process). *Confidence: High.* (Takeda sold its Dunboyne biologics plant to MSD.)

**AbbVie — Carrigtwohill, Cork — €60–63m (+€23m North Dublin hub)**
New sterile lines for the aesthetics portfolio; operational from 2025 with a 3-year new-technology training programme; ~70 jobs. The €23m North Dublin manufacturing-services hub is lower fit (not primary production). *Confidence: High.*

**Sanofi (Genzyme) — Waterford — €44m + permitted extension**
Biologics/rare-disease site (haemophilia — Altuviiio); €44m campus investment plus a newly permitted single-storey extension. **Added angle:** an FDA citation over Altuviiio manufacturing points to a potential data-integrity / CSV / quality-systems remediation opportunity. *Confidence: Med.*

**Ipsen — Blanchardstown, Dublin — €52m + €15m (2023–26)**
Oncology API site upgrade; +10% YoY API output; 175 staff. Modest but genuine in-window capex. *Confidence: Med.*
