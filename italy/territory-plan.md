## 2. Project detail

**Chiesi Nerviano** - Former Carlo Erba site, 124,000 m2, closed 2024, bought by Chiesi. Low-carbon inhalers plus sterile biologics. About 300 jobs by 2029. Mayor expects first activity in 2026, full operation by 2030. Digital scope not stated.

**Chiesi Parma Biotech Center** - Monoclonals, enzymes, proteins, from cell culture to packaging. Build began Nov 2022, operational from 2024 per company.

**Lilly Sesto Fiorentino** - New production lot on an 85,000 m2 campus (1,500+ staff, insulin hub) for diabetes and obesity drugs. Lilly reported first stone laid. Start-up date not found.

**GSK Parma / Tuscany** - Parma makes specialised antitumor, antiviral and specialty drugs from clinical to commercial. Rosia is the vaccine plant. Only totals are public.

**Menarini Sesto** - Oral solid dose plant, about 100m packs/year, described as smart manufacturing with automation and digitalisation. Status unknown.

**Sanofi Anagni / Scoppito** - Anagni moves to biologics; NFD2 freeze-drying department (2023) is highly automated. Scoppito has a high-potency workshop (Columbus) and a 2024 automation spend.

**Novartis Torre Annunziata** - FDA-approved site growing from 100m to about 150m packs; ZES-backed expansion. New area opened Feb 2024.

## 4. Customer approach (hooks)

- **Annex 11 / data integrity (CSV, MES):** best hook where new systems are being introduced: Chiesi Nerviano (new site), Sanofi Anagni (biologics + digital), Lilly Sesto (new building).
- **NIS2 (OT cybersecurity, lifecycle):** hook for all, and strongest at sites with existing live OT such as Novartis, Menarini, Chiesi Parma. Check whether each company is in NIS2 scope in Italy before leading with it.
- **Entry angles:** Chiesi - early architecture/CSV strategy for Nerviano before vendors are locked. Sanofi - digital-integration review of the NFD2 pattern for biologics lines. GSK - ask for the 2025-27 project list at Parma. Lilly/Menarini - first confirm status, then pitch commissioning and lifecycle support.
- **Technology stacks (job-posting search, thin).** Italian-site postings are scarce, so treat all of this as leads, not confirmed stacks:
  - Novartis: OT Automation Engineer postings name SCADA, Historian, MES, OPC, MQTT, GxP. City not stated, so it may not be Torre Annunziata. It is a clear IT/OT hook.
  - Lilly: an MES Architect posting (site unconfirmed) lists PharmaSuite, PAS-X, Syncade, DeltaV plus Annex 11 and GAMP 5.
  - Sanofi: group stack is Siemens Opcenter and Koerber PAS-X with PI and Ignition (US postings). Italian sites not confirmed. Scoppito is already paper-free and heavily digital.
  - Chiesi, Menarini, GSK Italy: no automation or MES stack found.
  - Mention of PAS-X: confirm Orise's PAS-X capability before using it as a hook.
- **Permits:** nothing public found for Chiesi Nerviano (VIA/AIA/permesso). Menarini Sesto last seen at the permit-request stage in 2022. Both need the municipal albo pretorio/SUE checked in a browser.
